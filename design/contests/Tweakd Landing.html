The problem

Right now, your car life is in several different places.

Every one of them was built for something else, and none of them talk to each other.

Facebook group
3,000 members, no search worth using, and the good thread is buried under a for-sale post.
A forum from 2007
The best technical knowledge on the internet, on a site that breaks on a phone.
Four Discord servers
Two are dead. Anything useful scrolled past three weeks ago.
Instagram
Great photos of your car, mixed in with everything that isn't your car.
WhatsApp thread
Where the meet is organised, if you happen to know someone already in it.
Camera roll
Four thousand photos. No build order, no dates, no idea which turbo that was.
A folder of receipts
The entire service history of the car, in a glovebox, in the wrong order.
The knowledge disappears, the people scatter, and every new owner starts from zero.
The solution

One app, built for one thing.

Everything a car enthusiast does online, in a single place that was designed for cars from the ground up.

Garage

Your cars, properly specified

Power, torque, weight, drivetrain, engine code, mileage and a full gallery.

Build log

Every mod, dated

Parts, cost and before/after gains, in the order they happened.

History

Service records that stay with the car

And transfer to the next owner when you sell, instead of ending up in a bin.

Marketplace

Sell the car with everything behind it

Not a listings site with a million ads — a small marketplace for enthusiasts. List a car straight from your garage and the whole record goes with it: mods, service history, events attended, contests won, and vouching from people the community already trusts.

Feed

Car content only

People with similar interests, cars you like, mods, events, and many more.

Forums

Answers that stay findable

Organised by brand, model and topic — so a question asked today still helps someone in 2029.

Meets

Everything happening, on one map

Meets, drives, track days and the shops worth the drive. Open an event to see how many people are going, who's bringing what car and which mods it's running, plus the contests on the day.

And more as the beta grows — the roadmap is shaped by the people in it.
